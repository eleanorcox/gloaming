# Gloaming

Gloaming is the working name. A field of fireflies at dusk, likely responding to sound and to people. The idea is still loose: nothing is built, the stack is not chosen, and the most useful work now is trying things out. Read [docs/vision.md](docs/vision.md) and [docs/open-questions.md](docs/open-questions.md) before planning or building anything.

How we work, git, writing and subagent rules are shared by all the projects and live in [../CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md). This file adds what is specific to Gloaming.

## Rules that already hold

- **Experiments are allowed to be rough.** Quick trials to find out what feels good go on an experiment branch, and what they taught is written into [docs/ideas.md](docs/ideas.md) before the branch is deleted. Test-first applies once something is being built to keep.
- **Biology is unchecked until sourced.** Facts about fireflies and glow-worms are labelled unchecked until there is a source.
